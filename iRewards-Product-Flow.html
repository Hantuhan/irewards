iRewards

The Smart Table Storefront That Brings Customers Back

Product Flow & Business Proposal

Target Market: Independent Cafes & F&B Merchants — Malaysia & Singapore

September 2026

Executive Summary

iRewards is a multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform that gives every table its own intelligent mobile storefront — plus an automated WhatsApp engine that brings customers back.

Customers scan a table QR and land in the iRewards storefront instantly — no app, no signup, no OTP. They order, pay via DuitNow / TNG / SGQR, and benefit from smart upselling and downselling in-app.

Rewards are optional. After payment, diners may join the merchant's iRewards club on WhatsApp (+1 point). Members receive automated bounce-back vouchers, review nudges, and churn win-back messages.

Merchants keep their existing POS. iRewards is the table storefront and retention engine. Customer data belongs to the merchant. RM199 / SGD 80 per month. No transaction commission.

Product Flow — Co-Founder Approved

Menu-first for ordering. WhatsApp for identity and retention after payment.

The iRewards Infinite Cash Loop

  1. Scan → Storefront → Order — Instant web menu. Returning members see "Your Usual." Upsell/downsell in real time.
  2. Pay → Optional Join — DuitNow/TNG/SGQR checkout. Post-payment WhatsApp join (+1 point). Guests who skip simply leave.
  3. Reputation Booster — 30 min after payment, members get a WhatsApp review nudge. Happy → Google link. Complaints → private.
  4. Retention Lock — Bounce-back voucher on checkout. 14-day expiry. Churn win-back if they don't return. Loop restarts on next scan.

Core Capabilities

  • iRewards Storefront — QR per table (?table=N). Full ordering channel.
  • Intelligent Upselling — Add-on suggestions when basket has room to grow.
  • Intelligent Downselling — Right-fit alternatives when customer hesitates or item is sold out.
  • Optional WhatsApp Rewards — Post-payment opt-in. Phone = loyalty ID.
  • "Your Usual" for iRewards — Favorite order pinned, one-tap reorder.
  • Local Cashless Payments — DuitNow / TNG / SGQR. Orders to kitchen via WebSockets.
  • Automated Post-Visit Engine — Vouchers, reviews, churn win-back on WhatsApp.

Core Problem

  • Labor shortage — Short-staffed teams, slow checkout, missed second orders.
  • Uncaptured revenue — RM1,500–4,000/month per shop in lost add-on sales.
  • 30% aggregator trap — Delivery platforms drain dine-in margins.
  • Generic QR menus fail — PDFs, OTP walls, high abandonment, no retention.

Business Model

  • Pricing: RM199 / SGD 80 per outlet per month (flat, no transaction fee)
  • Positioning: Table storefront overlay — does not replace counter POS
  • Data: Customer data belongs to the merchant; iRewards is the processor
